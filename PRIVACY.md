# Privacy Policy for Download Video / Reel Facebook

**Last updated:** August 29, 2026

**Download Video / Reel Facebook** (developed by Bin.Late) is committed to protecting your privacy.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, user activity, browsing history, or Facebook account credentials.
- **Local Operation:** All video extraction, processing, and WebAssembly media merging occur 100% locally on your computer inside your browser.

### 2. Permissions Justification
- downloads: Used solely to save the requested MP4 media files to your computer's default downloads directory.
- storage: Used to save temporary local user preferences and UI states.
- offscreen: Used to run local FFmpeg WebAssembly (WASM) to merge separated video and audio tracks requested by the user.
- webRequest / host_permissions (*.facebook.com, *.fbcdn.net, *.fbsbx.com): Used strictly to identify and fetch user-initiated video stream URLs directly from Facebook CDN.

### 3. Third-Party Services
- We do not use third-party tracking, analytics, or external cloud storage.
